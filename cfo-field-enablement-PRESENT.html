OBJECTIONS · REFRAMES

Top CFO Objections & how to reframe them

"We already have Microsoft Copilot / Fabric"

Reframe: Snowflake is the data foundation. Copilot is the productivity layer on top. Copilot without unified, governed data is expensive autocomplete. Show: Sources → Snowflake → Copilot.

"We already have an EPM (Anaplan/SAP BPC)"

Reframe: EPM manages the planning process. Snowflake makes the data underneath trustworthy, real-time, and AI-ready. SnowPlan can replace — or Snowflake enriches the EPM they keep.

"Our CFO is data-sceptical"

Approach: Lead with account-specific numbers. Show ONE compelling use case. Prepare a 'first 180 days' timeline. Have a reference the CFO can call. Honesty > polish.

"The AI might hallucinate on financial data"

Answer: Acknowledge it. Then: data grounding, Verified Queries, confidence thresholds, audit logs. "The AI surfaces the question. You make the decision. The data is yours, governed, auditable."
